The difference

Their AI stops at build time. Ours keeps working.

Nearly every “AI form builder” runs its model once, at design time, to generate a form for you. After that it is an ordinary form, and your lead still answers every question by hand. That second half is the half your lead actually feels.

How it works

Three steps, and one of them is paste.

1

Describe it, or draw it

Tell the copilot what you need to know from a prospect and it builds the form. Rearrange it on the visual canvas whenever you like — drag a question in, point a branch somewhere else, undo it if you were wrong. Qualified.at picks the right control for each question, from radio buttons to sliders, whichever moves the lead through fastest.

2

Add one script tag

Paste one script tag into the site you already have — WordPress, Squarespace, plain HTML, anything. The widget can open when a visitor clicks a button like “Estimate My Fee”, or on its own after a delay. Your personal snippet is waiting in the dashboard when you sign up.

3

Receive qualified leads

Leads answer qualifying questions right on your site. The AI parses free-text answers into structured data. You get a pre-qualified lead with answers, a complexity tier and a fee range — not just a name and an email.
