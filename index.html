The difference

Their AI stops at build time.
Ours keeps working.

Nearly every “AI form builder” runs its model once, at design time, to generate a form for you. After that it is an ordinary form, and your lead still answers every question by hand. Qualified.at has a copilot that builds the form too — and then runs the model again every time a lead arrives, so the AI works for them as well as for you. That second half is the half your lead actually feels.

Typical AI form builder

  1. 1 AI runs here. You describe the form you want; it drafts the fields.
  2. 2 You edit and publish it. The model's job is finished.
  3. 3 Your lead lands on a static form and answers all 17 questions, one by one.
  4. 4 You get a submission. Someone still has to read it and work out what it's worth.

Qualified.at

  1. 1 AI runs here too. Describe what you need to know; the copilot draws the flow, and you adjust it on the canvas.
  2. 2 Your lead types one paragraph in their own words.
  3. 3 AI runs here. It extracts the structured answers and skips every question they already answered — 17 questions become four.
  4. 4 You get a scored, tiered lead with a price estimate attached.

A graph, not a list

You draw a branching graph, not a page of fields, and there is no ceiling on how involved it gets — branches inside branches, paths that rejoin, whole interviews that only some people ever see. A simple filer answers eight questions; a complex one answers seventeen, from the same form. Nobody meets a question that doesn't apply to them.

An estimate, not a submission

Pricing rules live in the flow, so the lead sees a real range at the end and you see the breakdown — plus a silent complexity tier that routes them to the right person.

Qualifies without asking who they are

Counts, bands and categories — never identifiers. You can scope and price the work before a name or an email is ever requested, which is also why these forms convert.

The engine is open source

Flows run on the MIT-licensed Inquirex gems, free forever. Your flow definitions are portable text you can export and self-host. No lock-in.

How it works

Three steps from sign-up to qualified leads flowing into your pipeline.

1

Describe it, or draw it

Tell the copilot what you need to know from a prospect and it builds the form. Rearrange it on the visual canvas whenever you like — drag a question in, point a branch somewhere else, undo it if you were wrong. Qualified.at picks the right control for each question, from radio buttons to dropdowns to sliders, whichever moves the lead through fastest.

2

Add one script tag

Paste one script tag into the site you already have — WordPress, Squarespace, plain HTML, anything. The widget can open when a visitor clicks a button like “Estimate My Fee”, or on its own after a delay. Your personal snippet is waiting in the dashboard when you sign up.

3

Receive qualified leads

Leads answer qualifying questions right on your site. AI parses free-text answers into structured data. You get a pre-qualified lead with answers, not just a name and email.
